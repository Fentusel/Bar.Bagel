A BAGEL is round bread with a distinct chewy texture and golden crust, known for its satisfying bite and versatility. Originally created in Eastern Europe, BAGEL have become popular worldwide as a staple food that works just as well for breakfast as it does for lunch, dinner, or a late-night snack. They can be filled with anything from cream cheese and smoked salmon to eggs, meats, or sweet spreads, making them one of the most adaptable breads out there.
